# Appointment Companion product versioning

Visible releases use exactly two numeric components: **v2.67, v2.68, v2.69, ...**. The top-left status, About, app shell, specialist entry points and manifest must display the same complete product version. Do not expose a third patch component or truncate a product version in the status indicator. Internal technical source/test filenames may retain independent three-part identifiers. Historical changelog versions remain unchanged.
